Features

Your streaming services and your own files, browsed as one.

MyTaste shows what you can actually watch right now: the movies and shows on the subscriptions you pay for, mixed with the videos in your own folders. One grid, one order, one set of filters.

  • Your services, one catalog

    Choose your country and the subscriptions you already have, such as Netflix, Prime Video, or Apple TV+. You only see titles that are available on them, with posters, trailers, and cast from TMDB.

  • Your folders, mixed in

    Point it at the folders you already have: a USB drive, a NAS share, the same ones Plex scans. Files are matched on TMDB and appear in the same grid, in the same order, with a small badge.

  • Collections

    Popular, Latest, and genres out of the box, plus lists of your own. A list tells you how many of its titles you can watch right now, for example “12 of 23 titles are on your services”.

  • Plex-style filters

    Year, rating, genre, runtime, country, language, keywords, and people; for local files also resolution, codec, audio and subtitle language. A filter means the same thing for every source.

  • A player for your files

    Direct play when the browser can, ffmpeg when it cannot. Subtitles next to the file or inside it, resume where you stopped, Continue watching, and links that open an exact episode.

  • Yours to host

    A small Python app with a single SQLite file. No accounts, no cloud, no tracking. Run it natively, with Docker, or on a Raspberry Pi. Open source under the MIT License.

How it works

Up and running in a few minutes.

  1. Get a TMDB key

    Create a free account on TMDB and copy an API key or Read Access Token from the API section of its settings. MyTaste uses it for metadata and for what is streaming in your country.

  2. Install and start

    Python 3.11 or newer and uv are all you need. There is also a Docker image recipe in the repository.

    git clone https://github.com/RUverse/my-taste.git
cd my-taste && uv sync
export MYTASTE_TMDB_TOKEN=your-token
uv run mytaste serve

    Then open http://127.0.0.1:8000.

  3. Pick your services and folders

    Choose your region and subscriptions on the Services page. Optionally connect a folder of movies or shows; MyTaste scans and matches it in the background, and keeps rescanning on its own.

  4. Browse, filter, play

    Everything you can watch is in one place: by collection, with filters, in the same order TMDB would use, with your own files mixed in. Press Play on anything you own.
